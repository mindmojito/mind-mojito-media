// PRODUCTION FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

What is your typical project turnaround time? +
For fast-paced live events and concerts, our rush edit turnaround is 24 to 48 hours for viral social reels. For multi-camera podcasts and commercial brand films, delivery is typically 5 to 10 business days including revisions and sound design.
Do you travel for shoots outside Delhi NCR? +
Yes! We regularly produce and film across India — including Mumbai, Bangalore, Goa, Pune, and destination festivals. Our crew operates with travel-ready flight cases and modular cinema rigs.
What cinema gear and camera packages do you use? +
We shoot on Sony FX series and RED cinema cameras, equipped with prime cinema lenses, wireless video feeds, DJI Ronin stabilization, Aputure lighting fixtures, and multi-channel Sennheiser sound gear.
How does billing and booking work? +
We work with a 50% deposit upon contract signing to lock shooting dates and production crew, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery of final master files. Monthly retainer models are available for ongoing podcasts and brand social channels.
